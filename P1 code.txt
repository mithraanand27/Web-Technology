      <h2>About VOLT EV</h2>
      <p>
        VOLT EV is a fictional electric vehicle company that designs and builds
        zero-emission cars. Our aim is to make clean, quiet and powerful
        transportation available to everyone.
      </p>
      <p>
        This page introduces our vehicle models, battery technology, charging
        options and technical specifications.
      </p>

      <h3>What we stand for</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Mission:</strong> Accelerate the move to clean energy with vehicles people love to drive.</li>
        <li><strong>Vision:</strong> A future where every journey is emission-free.</li>
        <li><strong>Innovation:</strong> In-house battery, motor and software engineering.</li>
        <li><strong>Sustainability:</strong> Recycled materials and a battery take-back programme.</li>
      </ul>
